mission "Drak Revelations: Sagittarius A*"
	entering
	invisible
	source
		system "Sagittarius A*"
	destination "Earth"
	to offer
		has "drak revelations: sagittarius a*"
	on offer
		clear "drak revelations: sagittarius a*"
		conversation
			`The jump drive's effects have barely unveiled your ship when you freeze in panic: not only is the black hole much closer than you expected, it seems to be expanding so that in barely a second, its span covers your entire cockpit. You prepare to jump immediately and flee, but the <ship> is caught in Sagittarius A*'s oppressive gravity pull. In just a few seconds, you are pulled inside the maw at the center of the Milky Way, and everything goes black.`
			`	Against the dark background, you find yourself at the base of a seemingly endless staircase, formed of dust and ash. However infinite it may seem, somehow you can also perfectly make out the top, where it leads to a simple throne, which other than its shape is indistinguishable from the steps of the stairs.`
			`	You hear a commotion behind you, and turn to find a thousand forms, made of a bright light that shifts and stirs and reshapes itself for each of the beings, fighting one another. With each light extinguished, you find the staircase grows taller and taller.`
			`	Soon less than a dozen lights remain, but they are torn and splintered in their rays, and have seemingly grown weary of their war. They start fizzling out on their own, as if shut off by an invisible switch. After almost half have gone out, the other lights, wary and untrusting of one another as they may have been, rush to the center and fuse into one.`
			`	The being shifts wildly as it comes to, taking a hundred forms and one.`
			`	A tree. A human. An eye. A dog. A raven. An angel. A kestrel. A wyrm. An insect. A lion. A leaf. A lord. An infant. A vow. An end.`
			`	One, all, and none at once, the light stumbles past you, and begins the climb up the stairs. It seems barely able to keep itself together, struggling to remain in any one form at all as it takes its slow steps. It seems to keel over many times, only to find its footing at the last moment. After an eternity, it reaches the foot of the throne, and throws itself upon it.`
			`	The weight of relief and of the past washes over the scene, wiping away the staircase.`
			`	Just as it began with the black hole expanding, the vision is cut short as the darkness seems to shrink, and the light atop the throne shrinks and dims until Sagittarius A* is back at the size and position you expected it to be.`
				decline
